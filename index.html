Hi, I'm Mohammad. I came to Canada in 2019 and later studied Computer Science at Ontario Tech University, where I completed my bachelor's degree in 2025 with a concentration in Data Science.

My interest in analytics grew through university courses in database management, scientific data analysis, machine learning, information visualization, and statistics. I liked seeing how different tools could answer different parts of a question: SQL for organizing and querying data, Python and R for analysis, Excel for quick investigation, and Power BI for presenting the result clearly.

That interest became more practical through my projects. I've worked with retail sales and customer data, built Power BI and Excel dashboards, studied inflation data in R, analyzed scientific data in Python, and created a machine-learning model for wine-quality prediction. I enjoy the full process—from checking and cleaning the data to finding patterns and explaining what they mean.

I naturally ask questions when a result looks unusual. Why did one category lose money? Why did one region perform differently? Is the trend real, or is there a data-quality issue behind it? I like working through those questions carefully and turning the answer into something useful for a business decision.

My work at Loblaws and Estée Lauder has also given me experience with the day-to-day side of retail and production operations. I've worked around product quality, inventory, material movement, purchase orders, and SAP. That experience helps me understand that data comes from real processes and real people—not just spreadsheets.

I'm currently looking for a Data Analyst opportunity where I can bring together my technical skills, business curiosity, and practical operations experience. I want to keep learning, contribute to a team, and build analysis that people can actually use.
